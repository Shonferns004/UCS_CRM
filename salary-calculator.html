Salary Calculator

Upload the payroll Excel and it counts each employee's salary from the days worked (Present Days × Salary ÷ days in month), plus the full Gross → Net chain. Optionally pull the present days count straight from the database instead of the Excel column.
Auto-detects the current month's payroll sheet (latest date columns in the file). Formula used: Salary ÷ days in month × DB Present Days, where days in month come from the date columns in the sheet. DB Present Days from the Excel column = Present Days − Joining Deduction. When pulled from the database, it is the backend's final paid days: attendance present days minus absences, half-days, late-punch deduction, Sunday deductions, plus attended cancelled Sundays, minus the 1.5 joining deduction (already baked in). Then Gross = Month Salary + 10% Incentive + Aaj Ka Incentive + Weekly Incentive; Net = Gross + OT/Extra + Pending Expenses − Advance.
Columns highlighted in blue are calculated by this tool after upload; uncolored columns are read directly from the Excel file.
Present days will come from the Excel file until you pull them from the database (final paid days incl. absences, half-days, late & Sunday logic, joining deduction).
