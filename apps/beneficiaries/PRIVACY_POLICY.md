## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Being Sevak Charitable Trust** ("the Trust", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the **JOD / Beneficiaries** mobile application ("the App").

The App is a **field data-collection tool** used by authorised volunteers and operators ("Operators") of the Trust and its partner NGOs to register beneficiaries, record identity and disability documents, capture biometrics and photographs, and deliver welfare kits and programmes to persons with disabilities and other beneficiaries.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 3. Information we collect

The App is operated by an authorised Operator. Most of the personal data in the App relates to the **beneficiary** being registered or served, and some relates to the **Operator** themselves.

### 3.1 Operator (volunteer/user) information
- Full name, mobile number, email, and assigned operator/volunteer role
- Login credentials and an authentication token stored on the device
- Assigned area — city and state — used to pre-fill beneficiary records
- Biometric unlock (fingerprint / face) used **only** to secure the Operator's own device login through the operating system (e.g. Android BiometricPrompt). This biometric never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us.

### 3.2 Beneficiary personal information
- Full name, gender, date of birth
- Mobile number, occupation
- Residential address, city, state, PIN code
- Programme / needs information and the government or NGO programme details
- Disability details (type, percentage, certificate number, UDID number)

### 3.3 Identity and supporting documents
- Aadhaar card — front and back (Aadhaar number and card image)
- UDID card
- Disability certificate
- Other supporting documents captured during registration

### 3.4 Photographs
- A photograph of the beneficiary captured with the device camera

### 3.5 Biometric data (fingerprints)
- Fingerprint images, fingerprint templates, and capture payloads (FID/PID data) captured using a supported external USB fingerprint scanner (e.g. SecuGen Hamster Pro). Fingerprints are collected **only** for identity verification and de-duplication of beneficiaries, with the consent of the beneficiary, and are treated as **sensitive personal data**.

### 3.6 Location data
- Approximate and precise device location (GPS) associated with registration, distribution, and field activity. Location is used to verify that services were delivered at the correct place and to record programme coverage.

### 3.7 Aadhaar OCR processing
When an Operator captures an Aadhaar card, the card image may be sent to our secure backend server, which uses an AI vision service (Google Gemini) to automatically read and extract the fields printed on the card (name, date of birth, gender, address, Aadhaar number). The image is used only for extraction and is **not retained on your device** after processing.

### 3.8 Device and usage information
- Device type, operating system, and app version
- Notification and camera/storage permissions granted
- Basic technical logs, error reports, and security/tamper-detection signals
- Tokens and small preferences stored locally using the device's secure storage (SharedPreferences)

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, and the App does **not** contain third-party advertising.

## 4. How we use the information

We use the information to:

1. Register, verify, de-duplicate, and maintain beneficiary records
2. Confirm identity using documents and biometrics
3. Plan and deliver welfare programmes, kits, and distributions
4. Record proof of service and generate reports for the Trust, partner NGOs, and government welfare schemes
5. Authenticate Operators and secure their access to the App
6. Send operational notifications and updates
7. Prevent fraud, duplicate claims, misuse, and maintain data integrity
8. Comply with legal, regulatory, audit, and reporting obligations

---

## 5. Consent

- Beneficiary data is collected by the Operator **with the informed consent** of the beneficiary or their lawful guardian.
- Biometric (fingerprint), photograph, and location collection is carried out **only for the purposes described above** and is optional where the beneficiary declines, subject to the requirements of the relevant programme.
- Consent may be withdrawn by contacting us using the details in Section 14. Withdrawal may limit or prevent the delivery of certain services where the information is essential.

---

## 6. Sensitive data — Aadhaar and biometrics

We treat Aadhaar numbers and biometric data with heightened care:

- Access is limited to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.
- Data is transmitted over **HTTPS/TLS** encrypted connections to the backend (`api.beingsevak.org`).
- Fingerprint templates and biometric captures are stored securely and are **not shared for any commercial purpose**.
- Biometric data is used solely for beneficiary identity verification and de-duplication, and is not used for profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to welfare service delivery.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade Aadhaar or biometric data.

---

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

We do **not** sell personal data. We may share information only:

- **With the Trust and its partner NGOs / programmes** for which the beneficiary is registered
- **With government departments and welfare agencies** as required to deliver schemes, verify eligibility, and fulfil statutory reporting
- **With service providers** who process data on our behalf under contract, including:
  - Cloud hosting and storage providers for our backend
  - **Google (Gemini Vision)** for Aadhaar document text extraction
  - Communication providers (e.g. WhatsApp / SMS / email) for notifications and receipts
  - Biometric device SDKs used on-device for fingerprint capture
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of beneficiaries, the Trust, or the public.

All third-party processors are required to protect the data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

---

## 8. Data storage, security, and retention

- Data is primarily stored on our secure servers and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Some information (authentication token, operator profile, small preferences) is stored locally on the device.
- We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, encrypted transport, authentication, and audit logging.
- Personal data is retained **only as long as necessary** to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, to comply with legal and programme requirements, and to maintain accurate welfare records. When no longer required, data is securely deleted or anonymised.
- Operators must keep their device and login credentials secure and must not share beneficiary data outside the App.

## 9. Children and vulnerable persons

The App serves beneficiaries who may include children and persons with disabilities. Such data is collected **only by an authorised Operator with the consent of a parent, guardian, or lawful representative**, and is used strictly to deliver welfare services. We do not knowingly allow children to use the App independently — the App is operated by trained adult volunteers.
